Script Glow Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

This policy covers the Script Glow app for iPhone and Android. In short: Script Glow does not collect, store or share any information about you. Everything stays on your device.

No accounts

There is no sign-up or sign-in. The app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Your scripts and audio stay on your device

You bring a project into the app by opening a Script Glow backup file. The script text, character names, rehearsal settings and scene audio in that file are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. You can remove a project in the app with Remove from this phone, and deleting the app removes everything.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only if you choose Wait, go on when I stop talking. While the app waits for your line, it reads how loud the room is, so it can tell when you have finished speaking. It does not record, store, transcribe or send any audio. The microphone is released when you leave the scene.

No network, analytics or ads

The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it does not share data with third parties.

Children

Script Glow is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked by opening an issue at github.com/Mariano215/script-glow/issues.
